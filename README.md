
# Small Business SEO Checklist

SEO for small businesses is mostly a short list of things done consistently. Work through these in order.

Maintained by [Veloce Digital](https://velocedigital.co).

## Honest expectations
- New sites typically take months, not days, to rank for competitive searches.
- Nobody can guarantee a Page 1 ranking. Be cautious of anyone who does.
- Rankings follow three things: pages worth ranking, a site Google can crawl, and trust from other sites.

## Step 1: Make sure Google can index you
- [ ] Verify the site in Google Search Console
- [ ] Submit your sitemap
- [ ] Check the Pages report: how many pages are indexed, and why others are not
- [ ] Remove or `noindex` thin, near-duplicate pages. Fewer, stronger pages index better than many weak ones

## Step 2: Choose what to rank for
- [ ] Pick one main service and one main location or market
- [ ] Write down 5-10 phrases real customers would search
- [ ] Give each important phrase its own page; do not squeeze everything onto the homepage

## Step 3: Make each page worth ranking
- [ ] Title: the main phrase near the front, under ~60 characters
- [ ] Meta description: a clear reason to click, ~150 characters
- [ ] Real content: examples, prices or ranges, process, FAQs, proof (projects, reviews)
- [ ] Do not publish near-identical pages that only swap a city name

## Step 4: Local SEO
- [ ] Claim and complete your Google Business Profile
- [ ] Keep name, address and phone identical everywhere
- [ ] Collect genuine customer reviews and reply to them
- [ ] Add your business to relevant local and industry directories

## Step 5: Earn trust (backlinks)
- [ ] Ask clients for a "Website by ..." link in their footer
- [ ] Create profiles on legitimate directories and portfolio platforms
- [ ] Publish something worth citing: original data, a calculator, a useful guide
- [ ] Contribute expert quotes or guest articles to relevant sites
- [ ] Avoid bought links, link networks and mass directory submissions

## Step 6: Keep improving
- [ ] Monthly: review Search Console queries and pages
- [ ] Improve titles and descriptions of pages with impressions but few clicks
- [ ] Update key pages when facts change and show real update dates
- [ ] Track enquiries, not just rankings

## What to ignore
- Keyword stuffing and hidden text
- Guaranteed-ranking offers
- Submitting your site to hundreds of search engines
- Obsessing over daily ranking changes
